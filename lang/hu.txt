# colon, spaces included: some texts start or end with a space because the game joins them with other pieces, and the
# options of a pop-up box are the lines that start with a space. Escapes: \n a line break, \t a tab, \\ a backslash, \{
# and \} literal braces. Names in braces such as {GOLFER} or {MONEY} are filled in by the game.

# like the original; that text remains Electronic Arts'. Where a text comes from the original program, the comment above
# it names the routine or the string address in the publisher's golf.exe that the engine's code cites.
